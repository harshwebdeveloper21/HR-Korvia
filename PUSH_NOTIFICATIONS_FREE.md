# ✅ Push Notifications - 100% FREE Solution

## No Paid Services Required!

This push notification system is **completely FREE** and uses open-source technologies:

### What We Use (All FREE):

1. **VAPID (Voluntary Application Server Identification)**
   - ✅ Free, open standard
   - ✅ No registration required
   - ✅ No API keys from third parties
   - ✅ Just generate keys on your server

2. **Browser's Native Push API**
   - ✅ Built into Chrome, Firefox, Safari
   - ✅ No external services
   - ✅ Direct browser-to-server communication

3. **minishlink/web-push PHP Library**
   - ✅ Free, open-source library
   - ✅ Already in your `composer.json`
   - ✅ No paid license required

### What You DON'T Need:

- ❌ **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** - NOT needed
- ❌ **OneSignal** - NOT needed  
- ❌ **Pusher** - NOT needed
- ❌ **Any paid push notification service** - NOT needed
- ❌ **Monthly subscriptions** - NOT needed
- ❌ **Per-notification fees** - NOT needed
- ❌ **API keys from third parties** - NOT needed

### How It Works (Free):

```
Your Server (PHP) 
    ↓ (VAPID keys - free)
Browser Push Service (Chrome/Firefox/Safari - free)
    ↓
User's Mobile Device (free)
```

**Everything is free and runs on your own server!**
